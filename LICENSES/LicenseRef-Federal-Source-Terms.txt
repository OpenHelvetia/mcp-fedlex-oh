The files annotated with this identifier are recorded answers of public
endpoints of the Swiss Confederation, kept byte-frozen as test fixtures:

- Fedlex SPARQL endpoint (https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/sparqlendpoint),
  published by the Federal Chancellery; terms of use of the Fedlex
  platform apply (https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/de/legal-notice).
- LINDAS SPARQL endpoint (https://lindas.admin.ch/sparql), the politics
  cubes published under https://politics.ld.admin.ch/; terms of use of
  opendata.swiss / the respective publisher apply.

redistributed here solely so that the modules can be tested offline and
their results reproduced; re-recording is a deliberate, documented act.
